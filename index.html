About

I am an M.S. student in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Yohan Jo. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from SNU.

My research interests lie in natural language processing, particularly: (1) interpretability and model surgery of large language models through representation analysis, and (2) personalized alignment of large language models.
